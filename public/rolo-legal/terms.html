What Rolo does

Rolo helps you analyze, group, compare, and review photos in your Apple Photos library. It may provide quality or similarity suggestions and exact-duplicate results. Such results are aids, not guarantees that images are identical, poor, safe to delete, or backed up. You remain responsible for reviewing the full photo and its metadata before deciding what to keep or delete.

03 / 08

Your library and deletion

Rolo needs the Photos access you choose to grant. It only analyzes photos when you start a relevant action. A discard decision is reversible in Rolo until you confirm deletion. Actual deletion is requested from Apple Photos after your explicit confirmation and requires Apple’s system confirmation. Apple Photos and iCloud Photos control Recently Deleted, synchronization, and recovery. Check your backups and other devices before confirming, particularly for originals, RAW+JPEG pairs, and photos you cannot replace.

Pro access and annual trial

An active Pro entitlement is required before Rolo analyzes your photo library. Available options are monthly and annual auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. When available, eligible customers can start the annual subscription with a 7-day free trial that grants full Pro access. The monthly subscription and lifetime purchase have no free trial. Apple shows product availability, the exact local price, taxes, trial eligibility, and applicable terms before you confirm.

Subscriptions and lifetime

Monthly and annual subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel them in Apple Account subscription settings; deleting Rolo does not cancel a subscription. It is not a promise of future products, perpetual hosting, or features that do not yet exist. Purchases are associated with the Apple Account used to buy them and can be restored in Rolo where Apple and RevenueCat confirm the entitlement.
