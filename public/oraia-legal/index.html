Purchases and Premium

For missing access, use Restore Purchases in the app and check that you are using the Apple account that made the purchase. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Prayer history, intentions and journal entries remain on your device. You can erase them in Profile → Delete my data. This does not cancel a subscription. Read the privacy policy for details about purchases and service providers.
