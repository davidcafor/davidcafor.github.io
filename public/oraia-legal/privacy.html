Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Responsible developer and contact

Oraia is provided by an independent developer based in Spain. For privacy questions, rights requests or support, contact support. This policy covers the Oraia iPhone app. Public privacy and support information is also available at https://davidcafor.github.io/oraia-legal/.

Your private prayer content

Oraia does not require an account and has no advertisements or third-party product analytics. Prayer history, streaks, journal entries, intentions and preferences are stored locally. Oraia does not send this content, your selected faith tradition or your notes to the developer or RevenueCat. Your device backups may include local app data under your Apple or computer backup settings. Oraia does not provide its own cloud synchronisation service.

Purchases and technical information

Apple processes payments. Oraia uses RevenueCat to check Premium access, display products and restore purchases, including when the app checks access before a purchase. RevenueCat processes an app-generated pseudonymous identifier, purchase receipts and transaction information, entitlement status, and technical information such as device/operating-system and app versions. Network connections also disclose information such as IP addresses to the service receiving the request. A pseudonymous identifier is not a promise of complete anonymity.

The developer can access purchase and entitlement information through RevenueCat to operate and support purchases. RevenueCat’s dashboard also provides purchase statistics for this purpose. Payment-card details are not provided to the developer. Oraia does not send RevenueCat your name, email, journal entries, intentions or religious preferences, and does not enable advertising attribution or cross-app tracking. Apple and RevenueCat may process information under their own policies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Purposes and legal bases

Local preferences and records provide the features you choose. Purchase and entitlement information is used to provide the purchased service and restore access. Technical information is used to operate and secure purchase services and resolve faults. Where applicable, these purposes rely on performance of the service contract and legitimate interests in reliable, secure operation; legal obligations may require keeping certain transaction records. Optional reminder permission can be withdrawn in iOS Settings.

Reminders, widgets and sharing

Daily reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. Widgets and Live Activities can display prayer titles, progress and streaks on your Home or Lock Screen. You control these features through the app and iOS settings; people with access to your screen may see that information. If you export or share journal entries, the destination you choose receives them and its own privacy terms apply.

Support requests

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. These are used to respond and resolve your request. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or private prayer notes unless you intentionally want to share them. Messages are kept only as needed to handle the request and any applicable obligations. Email providers process correspondence under their terms.

Retention, deletion and subscriptions

You can erase prayer history, journal entries and intentions in Profile using “Delete my data”. This action does not cancel a subscription or erase records held by Apple, RevenueCat or an email provider. Removing the app normally removes its local app data, but copies may remain in your backups or files you exported. Manage those copies in their respective destinations. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account.

Purchase and technical records are retained by the relevant services as needed for their operation, restoration, fraud prevention and applicable legal obligations. For a request concerning data accessible to the developer, contact the email above. Oraia has no user account to delete.

Providers and international processing

Apple, RevenueCat and providers used for support or website hosting may process relevant information outside your country, including in the United States. Their policies describe their processing and transfer arrangements. Oraia does not sell personal data or share private prayer content for advertising. This policy does not claim that third-party providers receive no data.

Your rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact support. The developer cannot retrieve prayer content that remains solely on your device; use the in-app controls for that content. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority; in Spain, the AEPD: https://www.aepd.es/.

Children

Oraia is a general prayer app and is not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has sent personal data to support inappropriately, contact the developer so the request can be reviewed. Do not send sensitive information about a child unnecessarily.

Website and external links

The public support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages have no contact form, advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts. The hosting service may process technical request logs such as IP addresses under its own policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Opening an external link or sending email is subject to the destination's practices.

Changes

An updated policy will be published in the app and on the public privacy page with a revised date. Material changes will be reflected before the relevant new processing is introduced.
