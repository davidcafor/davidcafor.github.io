Oruvi for iPad

Frequently asked questions

How do I start?

Choose an artwork in the welcome collection. Select a numbered color and paint matching squares. Start with beginner artwork if you want a shorter introduction.

Can I use Apple Pencil?

Yes. You can paint with your finger or Apple Pencil, pinch to zoom and move around the canvas. Canvas settings let you adjust the experience.

What does Premium include?

The welcome collection includes six artworks. Optional Premium access unlocks the other collections. Available purchase options and their local prices are shown in the app before purchase.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings and choose Restore Purchases using the same Apple Account used for the purchase. An internet connection is needed to check your entitlement. If access is still missing, contact support with the product and approximate purchase date. Never send card details or your Apple password.

How do I manage a subscription or request a refund?

Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Deleting Oruvi or its data does not cancel subscriptions. Apple handles refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved on the device and can sync through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available. For sync issues, check that the devices use the same Apple Account and have an internet connection.

How do I delete my progress?

Use the delete-data action in Settings and complete both confirmations. This removes drawing records and resets settings; it does not cancel purchases or subscriptions. See the privacy policy for iCloud and purchase data.

How do I send an idea or report a problem?

Use Settings > Send Feedback, or email us below. Include your iPad model, iPadOS version, Oruvi version and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots are optional; remove any personal information before sending.
