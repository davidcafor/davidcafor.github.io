01. Who is responsible

Oruvi is developed by David Cardeña (davidcafor), based in Galicia, Spain. For privacy and support questions, contact davidcafor@icloud.com. This policy covers Oruvi for iPad and these support pages.

02. Drawing progress and preferences

The app stores drawing progress, painted cells, selected colors and settings on your device. Favorites and activity information support your collection, points and preferences. A local shared app container lets the Oruvi widget display a snapshot of your progress.

When iCloud is available, drawing progress can synchronize through your private Apple CloudKit database. This is not a public gallery. The developer does not have access to its contents through the CloudKit developer console. Apple processes iCloud data under its own privacy policy.

03. Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple handles payments. Oruvi does not receive your payment-card details. RevenueCat helps validate transactions, restore purchases, manage Premium access and display purchase options.

This involves an app-specific anonymous identifier, product and transaction information, purchase and subscription status, and the technical context needed to provide these services, such as app version, platform and locale. Oruvi does not require a separate account or send your name or email address as RevenueCat customer attributes. RevenueCat provides purchase-related reporting to the developer; this is not advertising tracking.

04. Feedback, sharing and links

If you email support, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. The feedback email is editable before you send it. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Sharing a finished artwork opens the system share sheet. Content is sent to the app or recipient you choose and is then subject to that service’s practices. Oruvi does not publish your artwork to a public feed. The Galicia link opens a fixed location in Apple Maps; Oruvi does not request your location for that link.

05. Why we use data

We process necessary app and purchase information to provide the features and access you request, including saving progress and restoring purchases. We use support correspondence to answer you. Under applicable European data protection law, these purposes rely on performing our agreement with you and, where relevant, legitimate interests in customer support, preventing fraud and understanding purchase performance. Information needed to comply with legal obligations is processed on that basis.

Oruvi does not sell personal data, serve third-party ads or use an advertising identifier. The app does not include a general gameplay analytics SDK. Apple may provide aggregate App Store metrics or diagnostics in accordance with your Apple settings; purchase reporting is provided by RevenueCat.

06. Service providers and international processing

The relevant services include Apple (App Store and iCloud), RevenueCat (purchases and entitlements), and the email services used when you contact support. These providers may process data outside your country, including the United States, under their applicable contractual and legal safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect rights and security.

07. Retention and deletion

Drawing progress is retained until you delete it. The app’s delete-data action removes stored drawing records and resets app preferences. Deletions can propagate to the private iCloud database when synchronization is available. You can also manage Oruvi’s iCloud data in Apple settings. Copies in device backups and already exported artwork are managed separately.

Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription, refund a purchase or erase Apple’s transaction records or RevenueCat’s purchase records. Those records support entitlement restoration, accounting and fraud prevention and follow the providers’ applicable retention requirements. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Contact support for a request concerning purchase-related data; we may need transaction information to locate an anonymous record.

Support correspondence is retained for as long as needed to resolve the request and relevant follow-up, or to meet applicable legal requirements and resolve disputes. Provider and hosting logs follow the respective providers’ retention policies.

08. Your rights and choices

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal data we hold, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting prior lawful processing. Contact the address above; we may ask for proportionate information to verify and locate your request. We cannot directly inspect or export the contents of your private iCloud database.

You can complain to your local data protection authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). Oruvi does not use personal data to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

09. Children

Oruvi does not offer public profiles, chat or third-party advertising. We do not ask children to provide contact details. Parents or guardians should supervise purchases and any feedback a child sends. If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should be removed, contact support.

10. This website and changes

These pages use no advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or external fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical request data, including IP addresses, for operation and security. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We will update this page when data practices change and revise the date shown above. Material changes will be communicated as required by applicable law.
