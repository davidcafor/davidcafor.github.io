Who is responsible

Rolo is developed by David Cardeña (davidcafor), based in Galicia, Spain. This policy covers the Rolo iPhone app and these pages. Contact davidcafor@icloud.com for privacy or support requests.

02 / 10

Photos and analysis

Rolo asks for access to your Apple Photos library. Granting access alone does not start an analysis: you choose when to analyze, review, compare, export, create an album, or prepare photos for deletion. Depending on the feature you use, Rolo reads photos, thumbnails, file resources, metadata such as dates and formats, and information needed to group or compare images. Quality suggestions, similarity checks, and exact-duplicate fingerprints are computed on the device. Rolo does not upload your photos or their metadata to a developer server. For some operations, an optional control lets you allow downloading originals from iCloud Photos through Apple’s Photos service; it is off by default.

03 / 10

Data saved on your device

Rolo saves preferences, the identifiers of photos used in the one-time 100-photo allowance, review decisions, session information, and analysis results or caches on your device. It uses these to resume your work, avoid repeating eligible processing, and enforce the free allowance. Apple Photos remains the source of your photo library. Rolo does not require a Rolo account. Local app data is generally removed when you delete the app, subject to device backups and platform behavior.

04 / 10

Deletion, exports, and sharing

Marking a photo for discard does not delete it. Rolo requests deletion only after you confirm in its discard tray, and Apple Photos presents its own confirmation. Deletions may sync to your other devices through iCloud Photos; photos normally move to Recently Deleted under Apple’s rules. Review selections carefully and keep your own backups. Exports and shares use Apple’s system controls and send content only to the destination you choose. Exported files and copies shared elsewhere follow that destination’s practices.

05 / 10

Purchases and RevenueCat

The first 100 distinct photos you analyze are available without payment; removing a photo does not restore an allowance slot. Monthly, annual, and lifetime Pro options unlock continued use. Apple processes payments and does not give Rolo your payment-card details. RevenueCat receives the purchase information and technical data needed to present products, validate transactions, restore purchases, and manage entitlements, including an app-specific anonymous identifier, product and transaction information, and purchase status. Rolo does not send your photos to RevenueCat or provide your name or email as customer attributes. RevenueCat supplies purchase-related reporting to the developer. See Apple Privacy and RevenueCat Privacy.

06 / 10

Support and legal basis

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use app and purchase data to provide requested functions and fulfill our agreement with you. We use support correspondence to answer requests and, where applicable, rely on legitimate interests in support and security. Information needed for legal or accounting obligations is processed for those obligations. Rolo has no third-party advertising or general-purpose analytics SDK and does not sell personal data. Apple may provide aggregate App Store metrics or diagnostics under your Apple settings.

07 / 10

Providers and transfers

Apple provides Photos, iCloud Photos when enabled, App Store purchases, and system sharing. RevenueCat manages purchase access. Email providers handle messages you send us, and GitHub Pages hosts this website. These providers may process technical or transaction information outside your country, including in the United States, under their applicable legal safeguards. We may also disclose information where law requires it.

08 / 10

Retention and your choices

On-device Rolo data remains until you remove it in the app where a relevant control exists, reset or delete the app, or the system clears it. Deleting the app does not undo an Apple Photos deletion, cancel a subscription, or erase Apple or RevenueCat transaction records. Purchase records are kept according to provider, accounting, and fraud-prevention requirements. Support messages are kept as long as reasonably needed to resolve your request and legal obligations. You can revoke Photos access in iPhone Settings and manage subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of personal data we hold, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact the address above; we may request proportionate information to locate and verify your request. You can complain to a data protection authority, including the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD). Rolo does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.
